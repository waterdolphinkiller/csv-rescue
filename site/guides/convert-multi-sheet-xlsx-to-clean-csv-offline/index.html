Step-by-Step Local Repair Workflow

  1. Drag your .xlsx workbook directly onto CSV Rescue in the browser (or run `node apps/cli/csv-rescue.mjs diagnose file.xlsx --sheet US_Orders` in Node.js 18+).
  2. Use the Sheet Switcher dropdown in the top file bar to inspect any tab in the workbook.
  3. Run diagnostics and apply deterministic cleaning rules (trim, deduplicate, normalize dates/phones/currency) with live Health Score feedback.
  4. In Safe Export, leave OWASP Formula Injection Protection enabled (prefix_quote or prefix_tab) and download your clean UTF-8 BOM CSV, standard CSV, TSV, or JSON.
Honest boundary: CSV Rescue’s zero-dependency .xlsx reader targets standard OOXML spreadsheets (shared strings, inline strings, numbers, booleans, and cached/raw formula values). Password-encrypted workbooks and legacy binary .xls (BIFF8) files should be decrypted or saved as .xlsx/.csv first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does CSV Rescue need SheetJS or heavy third-party libraries to read .xlsx files?

No. CSV Rescue parses ZIP central directories and OOXML worksheets natively using DecompressionStream("deflate-raw") in modern browsers and built-in node:zlib in Node.js 18+.

What is OWASP CSV Formula Injection and why does it matter?

Cells starting with =, +, -, or @ can be executed as formulas when opened in Excel or LibreOffice. CSV Rescue flags risky cells (while allowing harmless negative numbers like -42.50 and international phones like +1 415-555-0198) and offers optional prefix escaping on export.

Can AI agents inspect multi-sheet .xlsx files through CSV Rescue MCP?

Yes. Pass sheetName to csv_inspect, csv_preview_pipeline, or csv_execute_pipeline and the MCP server reads the requested worksheet directly inside your sandboxed directory.
