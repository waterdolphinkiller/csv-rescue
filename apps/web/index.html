Common CSV & Excel Repair Questions (US Operations, Privacy & AI Agents)

Does CSV Rescue upload my customer or financial data?

No. Every byte is decoded, diagnosed, repaired, and exported inside your own browser tab or local Node.js process. There is no backend server, no account signup, and zero external npm runtime dependencies.

Why does Excel drop leading zeros in US ZIP codes (02138, 07030)?

When you double-click a raw CSV, Excel auto-casts numeric strings into integers, stripping leading zeros from New England, New Jersey, and Puerto Rico postal codes. CSV Rescue detects at-risk postal columns, pads 5-digit and ZIP+4 codes safely as text, and exports Excel-ready SpreadsheetML (.xls) or UTF-8 BOM CSV.

How does CSV Rescue work with AI agents (MCP)?

Run node apps/mcp-server/index.mjs --allow-dir ./data to give Claude Desktop, Cursor, or Gemini CLI four deterministic tools (csv_inspect, csv_preview_pipeline, csv_execute_pipeline, csv_diff_or_merge). Symlink-safe path sandboxing locks disk access to your chosen folder, and any ambiguous rows can be exported to an offline Human-in-the-Loop HTML review page.
